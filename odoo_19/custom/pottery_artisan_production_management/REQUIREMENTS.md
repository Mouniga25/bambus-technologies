# Village Pottery & Artisan Production Management System

## Project Overview
An Odoo 19 custom module developed to manage pottery products,
artisans, raw materials, production, quality, customer orders,
deliveries, payments, and settlements.

## Main Features
- Artisan Management
- Product & Raw Material Management
- Production Workflow
- Kiln Management
- Quality Check & Recovery
- Customer Orders
- Delivery & Payment Tracking
- PDF and XLSX Reports
- Customer Website & Order Tracking

## Website
Home:
http://localhost:10511/pottery

Order Tracking:
http://localhost:10511/pottery/track-order

Start the Odoo server and install or upgrade the module:

pottery_artisan_production_management
